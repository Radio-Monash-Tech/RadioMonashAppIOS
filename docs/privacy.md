# Radapp Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 6 November 2026*

Radapp is the iOS app for Radio Monash. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is kept and which services it connects to.

## The short version

Radapp has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. It does not collect personal information, and nothing you do in the app is sent to Radio Monash or anyone else.

## Stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

- liked songs and recently played tracks
- saved and downloaded articles
- listening statistics
- followed shows and your settings
- cached cover art

This information is never uploaded. It is removed when you delete the app, and cached artwork can be cleared at any time in **Settings → Storage**.

## On-device features

- **Live captions** are made on your device from the stream's audio. The app does not use the microphone. The first time you turn captions on, iOS may download a speech model from Apple.
- **Show reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app asks for notification permission only when you follow a show.

## Services the app connects to

To load content, the app connects to these services. Like any website, they can see your device's IP address when it connects.

| Service | Used for | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Radiocult | The live stream, now playing and the schedule | Standard requests for the stream and station data |
| radiomonash.online | Journalism articles | Standard requests for article pages |
| Apple iTunes Search | Finding a song to share or play in another app | The song's title and artist |
| Songlink | Opened when you share a song or choose a streaming service | The song's Apple Music ID |

Each service has its own privacy policy.

## Children

Radapp is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
